# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome questions, critique ideas rather than people, and document technical disagreements with reproducible evidence.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and sexualized conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to `ethan.villalovoz@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed privately and handled in proportion to their severity. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
